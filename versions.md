# Funniest Cat Voicemail Greeting — 3 versions

Contest: u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-cat-voicemail-greeting-8eaad443
Gate: one line, 1–120 characters, first-person cat voicemail.
Judge order: (1) clearly a cat's voicemail + lands a laugh, (2) cat-specific joke,
(3) cat's own voice/attitude, (4) fast punch spoken aloud, (5) fresh wording.

## A — box + nap + food (109 chars)

You've reached the cat. I'm napping in the box the TV came in. Leave a message, or slide food under the door.

Why: most relatable image (every cat sleeps in the delivery box, not the gift);
closer turns the caller into room service. Setup is the longest of the three.

## B — knocking things off the shelf (115 chars) — recommended

You've reached the cat. I'm knocking things off the shelf. Leave a message after the beep. I'll knock that off too.

Why: purest cat logic — the voicemail message itself becomes one more thing
on the shelf to knock off. Punch lands on the last five words. Most original
twist of the three; no worn pun, no catchphrase.

## C — the vase confession (110 chars)

You've reached the cat. Leave a message after the beep. If it's about the vase, I did it, and I'd do it again.

Why: fastest laugh — clean voicemail frame, then a zero-remorse confession.
Most quotable last line. Closest cousin to B (both about knocking things off),
but the attitude here is defiance where B is mischief.
